This module is part of the AI module ecosystem and included in DXPR CMS.

AI Social Posts provides a flexible framework for creating and managing AI-assisted social media content across multiple platforms.

You need AI Social Posts if

  • You manage social media across multiple platforms and need content variations
  • You want AI assistance for creating platform-specific social content
  • You need to repurpose existing content into social media posts
  • You want interactive AI editing tools for refining social content

Features

  • Flexible Content Creation Paths:
    • Create posts directly from any content item via the "AI Social Posts" tab
    • Generate multiple platform variations from a single piece of content
    • Create standalone social posts from the central dashboard
    • Batch create posts across multiple platforms
  • Interactive AI Content Refinement:
    • Real-time AI editing commands directly in the editor
    • Quick actions like "Make Longer", "Make Shorter", "Simplify Language"
    • Context-aware content improvements
    • Generate new content from selections
    • One-click summarization
    • "Continue writing" functionality
    • Edit or review existing content
  • Platform Integrations:
    • X (Twitter) - 280 character microblogging
    • LinkedIn - Professional networking posts
    • LinkedIn Articles - Professional networking articles
    • Facebook - Social engagement posts
    • Medium - Long-form articles with formatting, with topical vibe check
    • Substack - Newsletter content with titles
    • Reddit - Subreddit-specific posts, with topical vibe check
    • Bluesky - 300 character social posts
    • YouTube - Video descriptions and metadata
    • Hacker News - HN posts, with topical vibe check
  • Extensible Architecture:
    • Easy addition of new social platforms through submodules
    • Fully fieldable post types for custom requirements
    • Customizable AI prompts per platform
    • Flexible validation and character limit rules

Post-Installation

  1. Install the base module and desired platform modules: 
    composer require drupal/ai_social_posts
  drush en ai_social_posts ai_social_posts_x ai_social_posts_linkedin
  2. Two ways to create social posts:
    • Primary: Navigate to any content item and use the "AI Social Posts" tab to create platform-specific variations
    • Alternative: Create standalone posts via /admin/content/ai-social-posts
  3. Customize AI generation prompts per platform to match your content strategy
  4. Optionally enable analysis tools for content optimization
