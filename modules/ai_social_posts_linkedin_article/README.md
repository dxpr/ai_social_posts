# AI Social Posts - LinkedIn Article

Provides LinkedIn Article integration for AI Social Posts.

## Features
- Separate title and content fields to match LinkedIn's article structure
- Support for long-form content with 110,000 character limit
- Rich text formatting capabilities
- Optimized for professional content with formatting options

## Differences from LinkedIn Posts
LinkedIn Articles support rich formatting options including:
- Headings
- Bold and italic text
- Bullet points and numbered lists
- Block quotes
- Code snippets
- Image embedding with captions
